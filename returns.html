Return & Exchange Policy

Customer satisfaction and food hygiene are our top priorities. Please review our clear guidelines below.

1. Damaged or Incorrect Deliveries

If you receive any package that was damaged during transit (e.g. broken seal, spilled sauce, or torn flour bag) or an incorrect item was delivered, please inform us on WhatsApp within 24 to 48 hours of delivery.

Simply send a clear photo or short video of the parcel and the shipping label to our WhatsApp support number: +92 318 3085519. We will arrange a replacement or resolve the issue promptly.

2. Food Safety & Hygiene Conditions

Due to strict health and food safety standards:

  • Perishable fresh items (such as eggs and mozzarella cheese) cannot be returned once delivered and inspected, unless reported as spoiled upon arrival.
  • Grocery packages (spices, pulses, flours, sauces) must be unopened, unadulterated, and in their original packaging to be eligible for exchange.

3. Exchange Process via WhatsApp

Because we use direct WhatsApp communication:

  1. Contact our support team on WhatsApp (+92 318 3085519) with your order summary.
  2. Provide your order details and photos of the item in question.
  3. Our representative will review and confirm pickup/replacement terms directly with you.

4. Refund & Replacement Timelines

Approved replacements are dispatched immediately or credited according to the agreement made between the customer and seller on WhatsApp.
