Shipping & Delivery Information

At ZentroMart, we strive to deliver your everyday essentials and commercial cooking supplies quickly and safely across Pakistan.

1. Nationwide Coverage

We deliver to all major metropolitan centers (Karachi, Lahore, Faisalabad, Rawalpindi, Islamabad, Multan, Gujranwala, Peshawar, Quetta) as well as tier-2 and tier-3 towns across all provinces of Pakistan.

2. Transparent Delivery Charges

Because our catalog contains diverse items ranging from lightweight spices (50g) to heavy commercial gallons (4 Litres) and whole wheat flour (10kg), delivery charges are calculated transparently based on total consignment weight and your exact destination city.

Note: Actual delivery charges will be confirmed and agreed upon with the customer via WhatsApp before the order is dispatched.

3. Packaging & Fragile Items

All flours, lentils, and powders are vacuum-sealed or poly-bagged to prevent moisture. Liquid items like 4-litre Delish sauces and synthetic vinegar are shipped in heavy-duty commercial jugs with secure anti-leak caps. Fresh eggs and mozzarella are packaged with special thermal and protective cushioning.

4. Estimated Delivery Times

  • Local / Same City Delivery: Typically within 24 to 48 hours following WhatsApp confirmation.
  • Major Cities: Approximately 2 to 4 business days via verified courier services.
  • Remote Regions: 3 to 6 business days depending on courier hub accessibility.

5. Order Tracking

Once your order is handed over to our courier partner, our team will message you the tracking number and delivery rider details directly on WhatsApp.
