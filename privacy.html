Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026. ZentroMart is committed to safeguarding your privacy through a static-first, zero-unnecessary-tracking approach.

1. Static Architecture & No Server Database

ZentroMart is hosted entirely as a static website on GitHub Pages. We do not operate server-side databases, SQL/NoSQL storage, Firebase, or runtime user accounts. You do not need to create an account, password, or profile to browse our catalog or prepare a cart.

2. Browser LocalStorage Usage

We use standard native browser LocalStorage exclusively for your convenience:

  • Remembering products you added to your Shopping Cart so they persist when you refresh or revisit the page.
  • Remembering products saved to your personal Wishlist.

This data never leaves your browser device and is not transmitted to any third-party marketing or tracking networks.

3. WhatsApp Checkout Communications

When you initiate checkout or a product inquiry, your browser prepares an encrypted WhatsApp link and opens WhatsApp directly. Any personal details you share during conversation (such as your delivery address or contact number) are communicated directly to the seller via WhatsApp's end-to-end encrypted messaging, governed by WhatsApp's Privacy Policy.

4. Payment Security

ZentroMart does not collect, process, or store credit card numbers, CVVs, or bank credentials on this website. Payment methods (Cash on Delivery or direct bank transfer) are verified directly on WhatsApp.

5. Contact Regarding Privacy

If you have questions regarding this policy, feel free to contact us on WhatsApp at +92 318 3085519.
